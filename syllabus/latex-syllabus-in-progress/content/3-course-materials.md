Copies of core readings will be provided to you via printed copies, web archives, and/or PDFs. Although there are no required books for this course, we will draw quite a bit from the following textbook:

- *Computing and Technology Ethics: Engaging through Science Fiction* by Emanuelle Burton, Judy Goldsmith, Nicholas Mattei, Cory Siler, and Sara-Jo Swiatek

Students will also make their own selections of different research papers, case studies, books, and other materials to present and discuss in class.
